# ✨ WoozOffline (Classic Nostalgic Virtual World)

A complete, 100% offline, standalone 2.5D isometric virtual world sandbox inspired by classic Flash-era **Woozworld** (circa 2010–2015).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **💎 Zero Paywalls & Infinite Sandbox**: 999,999 Wooz and 999,999 Beex with 100% unlocked clothing, hairs, accessories, and furniture catalog.
- **🎨 Iconic Color Wheel & Wardrobe Studio**:
  - 360° avatar rotation preview.
  - Huge collection of Hairstyles, Tops, Jackets, Pleated Skirts, Ripped Jeans, Platform Boots, Crowns, Fairy/Demon Wings, Headphones, and Expressions.
  - Mix & match colors on every garment zone with the authentic color wheel & palette swatch picker.
- **🗺️ Iconic Public World Hotspots**:
  - **Woozworld Central Plaza**: Paved avenues, lawn grass, marble fountain, flowerbeds, park benches, and stage.
  - **Club Wooz (Neon Rave)**: Animated multi-colored disco rave tiles, DJ Mixing Station, giant speaker stacks, and lounge booths.
  - **Runway Fashion Studio**: Red carpet runway, judge panel, spotlight beams, star podium, and trophy showcases.
  - **VIP Tropical Beach Resort**: Tropical waters, sun loungers, golden sand, palm trees, and beachside vibe.
- **🔨 Unitz Decorator & Room Builder**:
  - Custom Personal Penthouse sandbox.
  - Place 100+ furniture items with grid snapping.
  - Rotate furniture in 4 isometric directions (`R` key or button).
  - Elevate furniture up/down (`Q`/`E` keys or buttons).
  - Paint floor tiles (Hardwood, Marble, Checkerboard, Neon Disco, Lawn Grass, Red Carpet, Sand, Water).
  - Paint room wall colors and export/import Unitz setups via JSON.
- **🎭 Woozband NPCs & Simulated Social Life**:
  - Meet **MyaWooz** (Fashion Queen), **JennyWooz** (Social Hostess), **MaxWooz** (Techie & Gamer), and **JayWooz** (Master DJ).
  - Interactive dialogue trees, style tips, and music switching.
  - Simulated bot visitors roaming around, chatting in animated comic speech bubbles, and reacting to your messages.
- **🎮 Minigames & Activities**:
  - **Runway Fashion Show**: Step onto the runway star tile, match pose prompts before time runs out, score 10/10 from Mya, and celebrate with confetti and fanfare!
  - **Retro Arcade Trivia**: Test your knowledge of 2000s/2010s virtual world lore at the arcade cabinet.
- **🎶 Synthesized Retro Audio Engine**:
  - Built-in Web Audio API soundtrack (Plaza Pop, Club Rave, Runway Chic, Beach Grooves) and crisp sound effects for footsteps, bubble pops, furniture snaps, and emotes.
- **💾 Automatic Offline Persistence**: Saves your custom outfit, room designs, level, and stats in browser storage.

## 🕹️ Controls & Hotkeys

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| **Walk & Move** | Left-click on any isometric tile |
| **Sit on Chairs / Couches** | Click on any seat or sofa |
| **Interact with NPCs & Objects** | Click on Mya, Jay, Max, Jenny, DJ decks, lights, or arcade |
| **Rotate Placed Furniture** | `R` key or "Rotate" button |
| **Elevate Furniture Up / Down** | `E` (Up) / `Q` (Down) |
| **Delete / Store Furniture** | `Delete` or `X` key |
| **Zoom Camera In / Out** | Mouse scroll wheel |
| **Chat** | Type in bottom chat bar and press `Enter` |

---

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. Open a terminal in this directory:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
2. Open your browser at `http://localhost:3000` to start playing!
